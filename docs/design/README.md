# docs/design/

The UI design trail, in order:

- `01-design.md`: round-1 spec (goals, principles, layout, filter rules, URL/cookie model).
- `02-critique.md`: round-1 critique (23 findings, requirement check).
- `03-handoff.md`: **build spec.** Merges the owner's feedback and the critique; wins on any
  conflict.
- `04-critique.md`: post-build critique of the real app.
- `05-where-flow.md`: owner round 6: Where rethought so clubs are the only thing you pick, and
  nothing loads until you do. Supersedes the Where parts of the handoff.
- `06-critique.md`: critique of round 6 (the Where flow, the app link, the subscription copy).
- `mock/`: round-1 static mock board (`index.html`, frames `#f01`–`#f09`) and its screenshots in
  `mock/shots/`.
- `build-shots/`: screenshots of the built app's key states (phone, 320 px, desktop, light and
  dark), used by the post-build critique.
